# Honest Evaluation Results (regenerated — concept-leakage fixed)

Generated by `npm run benchmark` / `npm run benchmark:75-25` / `npm run track-record`.
Canonical fixtures: `src/generated/benchmark-results.json` (primary), `src/generated/benchmark-75-25.json` (appendix), `src/generated/track-record.json`.
Buyer-facing surfaces carry NO performance figures (positioning policy); engineering docs quote the fixtures.

## Validity note (read first)
Backtest concept sliders are neutral constants (clarity 6, novelty 5) in all harnesses —
the CSV contains no content scores, and deriving sliders from each test film's verdict
leaked the outcome into test inputs. The walk-forward therefore measures the data-driven
core only. Production evaluations add real user concept + pre-sale inputs (up to ~30% of
score weight) whose variance is unobservable in backtests.

## Primary Benchmark: Walk-Forward (Forward-Chaining)
Train < Y, test Y, rolling 14 folds (2012–2025). Full scoring engine (ML blend + Bayesian + 10 components). **658 films tested.** This is the headline metric.

| Metric | Value [95% CI] |
|--------|---------------|
| Accuracy | 29.3% [26.0%, 32.9%] |
| Greenlight Precision | 11.3% [6.8%, 18.1%] |
| Greenlight Recall | 18.7% [11.5%, 28.9%] |
| F1 Score | 14.1% [7.9%, 20.1%] |
| Greenlight Calls | 124/658 (14 hits of 75 total) |

Naive baselines (same folds, same scoring rule): always-flop **80.5%**, band-median-multiple **63.7%**,
GBM RMSE 1.303 vs band-median RMSE 1.230, train hit-rate prevalence **11.0%**.

## Threshold policy
Sweep over offsets −15…+20 proves precision is flat (~9–14%) at EVERY operating point —
scores barely rank hits above flops on data-driven signal alone, so no threshold manufactures
precision. Base `BASE_PCT_THRESHOLDS` retained: with precision flat, thresholds only trade
accuracy against recall, and base preserves the most recall (33.3%) at 31.9% GL calls.
Chasing accuracy toward always-flop would destroy the product's only function (finding hits).

## Considered and rejected (documented, not retried without new evidence)
- Confidence sample-weights: all 729 trainable films are High confidence (728) + 1 Medium — zero variance, no experiment to run.
- Isotonic calibration: with ~flat discrimination it maps everything to the base rate; the percentile-rank framing is already the honest claim.
- ML-blend reweighting: reopens a do-not-reargue item for likely noise.
- Per-band threshold tuning: overfit risk on tiny band-year cells.

## Track record: 2024–2025 holdout (train ≤2023, n=69)
Accuracy 20.3% [12.5–31.2] · precision 13.5% [5.9–28.0] · recall 55.6% · 37 GL calls (5 hits of 9).
Calibration at n=69 is thin at the low end (P75+: 9.1% [3.9–19.6], n=55; P60–74: 28.6%, n=14; lower buckets empty) — reported as-is. Per-film record: `docs/track-record.md`.

## Key Takeaways
- Fixing the concept leakage dropped walk-forward precision 21.8% → ~11% (≈ prevalence 11%). Prior numbers were inflated by perfect-foresight sliders; current numbers are the honest data-driven floor.
- Verdict–risk coherence guard: greenlights under high/very-high diagnosed risk are capped at conditional (stated in the narrative). Conservative direction only; applies identically in backtests and live evaluations.
- Percentile buckets recalibrated to the current engine (neutral-concept training distribution); recalibrate after any engine change that shifts scores.
- Imputed rows (236) excluded from training — their targets are deterministic band medians.
- Era-aware break-even (pre_ott/ott_growth/covid/mature) lowers normalized multiples for pre-2015 films (less rights coverage), shifting their percentile rankings.
- 9-dim feature vector (no genre OHE, no production_house in GBM) — no target encoding; genre excluded by construction.
- Log-space GBM target tried and reverted.

## Known Limitations
- Data-driven discrimination is weak: precision ≈ base rate across all thresholds. The model catches roughly a fifth of hits (recall 19%) but cannot separate them precisely without user concept + pre-sale inputs.
- 80.5% always-flop baseline dominates accuracy; regime-break years (2020–2021, 2022) and thin early folds are noisy.
- 2024–2025 holdout is small (n=69); calibration CIs are wide.

# Bollywood Investment Research (2025)

## Key Market Facts
- 2025 Indian box office: ₹13,395 Cr. Hindi cinema record: ₹5,504 Cr.
- 37 films crossed ₹100 Cr in 2025 (vs 22 in 2024).
- Top 10 concentration fell from 41% → 33% — the "long tail" is growing.
- Non-star-driven films (Chhaava, Saiyaara) succeeded — star power is declining.

## Academic Findings (ML Research)
- **Director has MORE predictive power than Lead Actor** — contrary to conventional wisdom.
- Key factors (ranked): Budget > Director > Cast popularity > Release timing > Genre.

## OTT/Satellite Market (Post-Pandemic)
- OTT uses **performance-linked pricing**: base (~40% budget) + slab increases.
- Big films recover 60–80% of budget pre-release through non-theatrical rights.
- Satellite rights collapsed to ~10% of budget (down from 30–50% pre-pandemic).
- OTT: 40–60% of budget. Music: 10–20%. Overseas: 10–25%. Brand: 5–15%.

## Theatrical Share (Distributor/Exhibitor Economics)
- Week 1 multiplex split: ~41% to distributor after GST.
- Producer's effective theatrical share: **35–40%** (NOT 50%).

## New Features (May 2026)
- **Continuous Outcome Model**: Replaced binary win-rate scoring with expected break-even-normalized multiple.
- **Production House Field**: Dropdown with 21 major houses; scored from dataset avg multiples (5% producer weight).
- **Genre Appetite Trajectory**: Green/red arrows next to genre scores showing trending up/down (3yr WR vs all-time).
- **Confidence Intervals**: ±range displayed in score cards (derived from component sample sizes).
- **Sensitivity Analysis**: All material score levers shown in results (upside gains + budget-overrun/downshift risk views); tested-but-negligible moves listed separately ("won't move the needle") — never silently dropped.
- **Pre-Sale Benchmarking**: Market ranges per budget for each rights category (below/within/above).
- **Inline Validation**: Required fields enforced before submit; data quality warnings.
- **Demo command**: `npm run demo` scores three frozen synthetic films end-to-end (see `docs/demo-output.md`).
- **Walk-Forward Validation**: Annual rolling backtest (14 folds, 658 films — see fixtures).

## Known Data Quality Issues
- Dataset survivorship bias: only 729/2454 films have both budget+gross; dataset median raw multiple 1.21× vs real market ~1.0×.
- All 8 content-score columns completely empty — user sliders (conceptClarity, novelty) are the only source of this signal.
- Pre-sale rights data does not exist in CSV — user-input only; backtest estimates from budget.
- Tier system methodology (`actor_tier_proxy`, `director_tier_proxy`) is a black box — pre-computed, undocumented.
